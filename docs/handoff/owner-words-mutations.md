# The owner's words: Grand Devouring and Potential Unlock (binding; quoted exactly)

Sent after 3.6.2 with three pictures (kept in `docs/handoff/owner-refs/`: `devouring-anime-still.webp`, a still of the kagune's mouth
over someone; `devouring-drawing-with-player.png`, the owner's drawing of the tentacle arched over and its mouth come down over a
player, his whole body inside it down to his feet; `devouring-drawing-shape.png`, the same tentacle's shape alone).

---

We're adding two new mutations to Noro's kagune: Grand Devouring and Potential Unlock. Both must integrate with the existing kagune system, animations, combat mechanics, energy system, and multiplayer synchronization. These are separate unlockable mutations, each with its own mechanics and functionality.

Mutation 1 - Grand Devouring

Grand Devouring is an automatic execution mutation that activates when a nearby entity or player reaches 1 heart (2 HP) or less.

When a valid target reaches this threshold, one randomly selected available tentacle automatically begins the execution.

The selected tentacle slowly rises above its normal position and begins dramatically expanding in thickness, length, and overall mass. Its mouth becomes enormous, stretching its jaws unnaturally wide and exposing multiple rows of sharp teeth.

This transformation must be progressive, with the tentacle visibly growing rather than instantly changing size.

Once fully expanded, the tentacle tracks its weakened victim and extends toward them, opening its massive mouth to engulf their entire body.

Grand Devouring must NOT be a fast execution. The entire process should be slow, disturbing, and deliberately animated. The victim should remain visibly trapped inside the mouth for several seconds before being swallowed.

The mouth must be capable of adapting to literally any entity size. Whether the target is a normal Minecraft player, a tiny mob, a giant, or an enormous modded creature, the mouth and surrounding tentacle must dynamically expand to accommodate the entire body.

The tentacle's thickness, mouth opening, jaw dimensions, and swallowing animation must scale proportionally to the victim's actual dimensions. The victim should never visibly clip through the mouth because of insufficient size.

When the victim is engulfed, the jaws gradually begin closing around them. Their body is slowly drawn deeper into the mouth until it completely disappears from outside view.

If the victim is another player, they must experience a special first-person perspective from inside the kagune's mouth.

The victim should be able to see the inside of the enormous fleshy cavity, including the thick organic walls, multiple rows of teeth, moving tissue, and the throat extending deeper into darkness.

While trapped, they should visibly witness the jaws slowly closing around them, gradually blocking the outside world.

The interior should feel like an actual living creature rather than an empty red room. The fleshy walls should pulsate, contract, and move around the victim while the mouth slowly pulls them deeper inside.

Include appropriate wet biological sounds, muffled exterior audio, jaw movements, and swallowing sounds.

The victim must remain stunned and completely immobilized throughout the capture and initial swallowing sequence. They cannot move or escape using ordinary movement controls.

Do not replace this experience with a black screen, static texture, or instant death animation. The victim must actually see the interior of the mouth before dying.

The execution only kills the victim after the engulfing and swallowing sequence reaches its final stage.

However, the execution animation does not stop when the victim dies.

Once the victim has been fully swallowed, an enormous visible bulge must form inside the tentacle, representing the swallowed body.

This bulge slowly travels along the ENTIRE length of the tentacle toward the player's back, exactly like a giant snake swallowing its prey.

The tentacle must physically expand around the moving bulge, with the fleshy exterior visibly stretching, contracting, and deforming as the swallowed body travels through it.

The bulge must also scale according to the victim's size. Swallowing a giant creature should produce a significantly larger bulge than swallowing a normal player.

The movement should be slow, smooth, and continuous. The swallowed body must not simply teleport into the player.

When the bulge finally reaches the base of the kagune at the player's back, it gradually disappears into the body, completing the digestion animation.

Only when digestion finishes does the player receive a significant restoration of their food/energy bar.

During the ENTIRE execution process, the tentacle performing Grand Devouring becomes completely unavailable for any other action.

It cannot attack, defend, grab, perform automatic attacks, or participate in ANY other kagune ability while it is executing and swallowing its victim.

This restriction lasts from the initial growth animation until the swallowed bulge has completely reached the player's body.

If the player has multiple tentacles, all remaining available tentacles must continue functioning normally.

For example, if the player has four tentacles and one is performing Grand Devouring, the other three remain available for combat and abilities.

Every kagune ability must account for tentacles currently occupied by executions. Abilities that can function with fewer tentacles should use the remaining available appendages. Abilities requiring a specific number of tentacles must become temporarily unavailable if insufficient appendages remain.

The executing tentacle must not be forcibly redirected or used by another ability before finishing its execution.

Once the digestion animation finishes, the tentacle gradually returns to its normal size and position, becoming fully functional again.

Ensure the entire process is synchronized in multiplayer. Other players must be able to see the tentacle expanding, capturing the victim, swallowing them, and the bulge traveling toward the user's body.

The victim's first-person experience must match the actual execution animation happening in the world.

Grand Devouring should feel like a terrifying biological feeding mechanism, not a conventional attack. Its defining characteristics are the enormous adaptive mouth, slow consumption, visible digestion, energy restoration, and temporary sacrifice of one functioning tentacle.

Mutation 2 - Potential Unlock

Potential Unlock is a separate mutation based on Noro's extraordinary regenerative capabilities, allowing the player to survive catastrophic bodily damage and physically reattach severed body parts during combat.

This mutation expands upon the already existing ability "Thought you'd get rid of me that quickly?". Reuse its existing regenerative materials, biological tissue effects, and animation systems wherever possible.

Upon unlocking Potential Unlock, the player's energy bar permanently changes color, visually representing the mutation's activation.

The maximum energy capacity is also permanently multiplied by 3. This means the player can store three times as much energy as before unlocking the mutation.

The increased maximum energy capacity must persist after death, relogging, and server restarts.

While the player still has energy remaining, they gain the ability to survive devastating injuries that would ordinarily cause death, including the physical destruction or separation of body parts.

During particularly violent fights, the player's limbs can be physically torn away by extremely powerful attacks.

Arms can be ripped off at the shoulders, legs can detach, pieces of the torso can be torn apart, and organs or other biological structures can become exposed or separated.

During exceptionally devastating attacks, even the player's head can be completely severed from the body.

However, dismemberment must NOT happen frequently.

It should only occur during extremely powerful impacts, potentially lethal blows, unusually devastating attacks, or situations where the damage realistically justifies losing a body part.

Ordinary attacks must not cause random dismemberment. The mechanic should remain relatively uncommon so that witnessing it during combat actually feels significant.

When a body part is detached, it must physically separate from the player and fall onto the ground.

Severed body parts must preserve the player's original Minecraft skin and corresponding body geometry.

They should react naturally to gravity and the impact that caused their separation, including appropriate movement and rotation while falling.

Once detached, the missing body part must actually disappear from the player's normal model until regeneration is complete. Do not render duplicate limbs or leave the original limb attached while its severed version is on the ground.

As long as the player has energy remaining, the body automatically begins reattaching the severed parts.

Every detached limb takes approximately 5 seconds to completely reattach.

This reattachment must be physically animated from beginning to end. Do not simply wait five seconds and teleport the body part back into position.

The regeneration begins with long, thin, organic strands of vein-like kagune tissue emerging from the exposed injury.

These strands should look like living tendons, blood vessels, and fleshy biological fibers.

They stretch outward from the player's body toward the severed limb, moving and twisting independently as they extend through the air.

When the strands reach the detached body part, they physically connect to it, wrapping around its exposed attachment area.

The strands then begin tightening and contracting, gradually lifting the detached limb off the ground and pulling it toward the player's body.

The severed body part must visibly travel through the air as the organic strands pull it closer.

During this movement, the limb should naturally rotate and adjust its orientation until it is correctly aligned with its original position.

As the body part approaches its attachment point, additional tissue grows between the two surfaces, visibly reconnecting the damaged biological structures.

The strands should continuously stretch, contract, pulsate, and tighten throughout this process.

Once the limb reaches its original position, the remaining flesh closes around the injury, the organic strands retract or merge into the body, and the original body part is fully restored.

The entire sequence must last approximately 5 seconds and be smoothly animated.

The same regeneration mechanics apply to the head, torso sections, and other severed body parts.

For example, if the player's head is torn off, the head must physically fall onto the ground while the body remains capable of continuing the regeneration process.

Organic kagune strands then extend from the exposed neck toward the detached head, connect to it, and gradually pull it back toward the body.

The head must visibly rise from the ground, rotate into the correct orientation, and reconnect to the neck through the same fleshy tissue animation.

The player's camera must remain functional during this entire process, including when the head is detached. Make sure first-person and third-person rendering remain consistent and do not cause camera clipping or broken perspectives.

Torso sections should similarly reconstruct through expanding and reconnecting layers of biological tissue.

If several body parts are severed simultaneously, each detached part must regenerate independently.

For example, the player could lose both arms during a devastating attack, causing two separate sets of organic strands to emerge from the shoulders and retrieve the arms simultaneously.

Every limb should have its own complete five-second reattachment animation rather than forcing all body parts to return together.

Every time a severed body part successfully reattaches, the player receives a small health boost.

The health restoration must occur only after the body part has completely reconnected, not when the regeneration begins.

Regeneration requires energy. While the player has sufficient energy, the reattachment process continues automatically.

If the player's energy runs out during regeneration, the process must be interrupted until sufficient energy becomes available.

Without energy, the mutation can no longer protect the player from otherwise lethal damage.

Potential Unlock should make the player extraordinarily difficult to kill during combat, but it should not provide unconditional invincibility.

The player must remain capable of fighting while body parts are regenerating.

If an arm is missing, the remaining arm and available kagune appendages should still function normally.

The animation system must account for missing limbs so that attacks do not incorrectly animate body parts that are currently detached.

If another powerful attack occurs while the player is regenerating, the system must handle it without duplicating severed limbs, breaking ongoing animations, or producing overlapping regeneration effects.

The visual regeneration materials must be reused from "Thought you'd get rid of me that quickly?", with one important difference.

All regenerative strands and biological tissue must inherit the player's currently selected kagune color.

Do not use a fixed red or any other hardcoded color. The existing kagune color customization system must determine the appearance of every regeneration strand.

The biological tissue should have smooth, organic movement, resembling living veins and tendons actively reconnecting the player's body.

Use GeckoLib and the existing animation systems to ensure the strands, severed limbs, and reconnection sequences are properly animated.

The visual result should resemble Noro's terrifying regeneration in Tokyo Ghoul, where the body appears capable of reconstructing itself despite seemingly fatal injuries.

The entire system must function in multiplayer.

Other players should be able to witness a limb being severed, falling onto the ground, organic tissue emerging from the wound, the tissue connecting to the severed limb, and the limb being physically pulled back into place.

All severed body parts, tissue animations, regeneration timing, health restoration, and energy consumption must remain synchronized between server and clients.

Potential Unlock and Grand Devouring must remain compatible with one another.

A player should be capable of regenerating severed limbs while their kagune tentacles continue fighting or performing Grand Devouring.

Neither mutation should interfere with the other's animations, energy mechanics, or combat functionality.

The final result should make Noro's kagune feel like a truly horrifying living organism. Grand Devouring demonstrates its ability to consume virtually anything, while Potential Unlock demonstrates its ability to reconstruct its own body after catastrophic destruction.

Implement both mutations as distinct unlockable features, reuse existing systems wherever appropriate, preserve all existing kagune color customization, and prioritize smooth biological animations over instant visual effects.



PS:

During Grand Devouring, the moment a victim is successfully captured inside Noro's mouth, they must be completely stripped of all combat capabilities, regardless of whether they are a player, wild ghoul, CCG investigator, or any other entity.

All active kagunes must immediately retract and become fully deactivated. This includes normal kagunes, Kakuja transformations, additional kagune appendages, and any active kagune abilities. No kagune geometry should remain visible on the victim while they are being swallowed.

For CCG investigators or players wielding quinques and other weapons, their weapons must immediately disappear from their hands and become completely unusable. Weapons must not be deleted from inventories; they should simply be temporarily hidden and disabled throughout the execution.

The victim must be unable to activate kagunes, transform, attack, use abilities, equip weapons, defend themselves, or perform any combat-related action while being devoured.

This restriction must apply equally to NPCs and players. Ghoul NPCs must stop using their kagunes entirely, and CCG investigators must stop using their quinques and combat AI.

All combat restrictions must activate immediately upon capture and remain active throughout the entire engulfing and swallowing animation.

If the execution is interrupted or cancelled before the victim dies, their previous equipment and combat capabilities must be restored correctly. If the execution completes, the victim dies normally, following the existing death and inventory rules.

This is extremely important visually. When Noro engulfs someone, their kagune must be COMPLETELY OFF, and their weapons must be gone from view. Nothing should clip through the mouth or remain sticking out of the tentacle while the victim is being swallowed.

The victim is completely helpless inside the mouth, with no active weapons, kagunes, transformations, or combat abilities. (FULL STUN)
