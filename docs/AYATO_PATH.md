# Path design: Ayato Kirishima — "Black Rabbit" (Ukaku)

HexKagune gets a **path choice** the first time a player's kagune is unlocked. The existing system becomes the
**Kaneki path** (Rinkaku → Centipede → Kakuja), unchanged. The new **Ayato path** is a Ukaku kit that copies Ayato.

## The canon-only rule
- **Every ability is something Ayato does on the page or on screen.** Each one cites the canon fact (C#) it copies.
  Nothing is invented: no move he never uses, no form he never takes.
- **The look follows his eras.** Early stages look like *Tokyo Ghoul* / *√A* Ayato; late stages look like *:re* Ayato.
- **What is necessarily ours:** Tokyo Ghoul never gives numbers or names for most moves. Damage, cooldowns, RC costs
  and the mastery thresholds are tuning, and move names are plain descriptions. *What* each move does is canon; *how
  much* is gameplay.
- **Not in this path, on purpose:**
  - **No kakuja.** Ayato never develops one in any canon source (C14). The path ends at fully mastered *:re* Ayato.
  - **No air dash, dive-bomb or gliding.** He is fast and agile in the air (C8), but Ukaku wings are not shown
    flying or propelling him, so speed and agility stay passive.
  - **No grapple, crush or slam.** Ukaku kagune never grab in canon.
  - **No lightning.** RC "lightning" belongs to other Ukaku users (Yomo, the Narukami quinque; C13), not Ayato.

## Look (by era)
- **Where it comes from:** two wings from the **shoulder blades**, one per shoulder (C2). It is not tendrils from
  the lower back.
- **Shape:** angel-like wings (C2) made of glimmering, sharp, blade-like RC (C17). Each wing has a leading edge that
  rises from the shoulder blade and sweeps out, with blade feathers hanging from it.
- **Colour and two states (C3, C4), matched to anime frames:**
  - **Gaseous (at rest):** RC flame. A molten red-orange core (`#FF5A14`, hot `#FFC24A`) inside a ragged red heart
    (`#E3161C`), dark crimson to purple-black flame tongues (`#2C0A21` → `#150611`) licking up and out, and a big
    pale pink aura (`#F6D2EE`) hugging the outline. It flickers constantly.
  - **Crystallized (to strike, block or fire):** jagged, saw-edged blades. Dark starry violet inside (`#1B1031`
    with pale blue star points), glowing red edges (`#FF2A24`), a pink rim (`#F5C4E6`) and a molten glow at the
    root.
  - **One-Winged Kagune (*:re*):** a single giant crescent scythe of faceted crimson crystal (`#D5121E`, lit facets
    `#FF3B30`, deep `#8E0A16`), dark inclusions, a bright cutting edge and thorny serrations along the outer curve.
  - Manga panels are red (C18); vermilion is the colour the sources give for Ayato in colour.
- **Era 1, *Tokyo Ghoul* / *√A* (mastery 0–749):** a pair of wings 2 to 2.7 blocks long that flare up and out
  above the head, growing a little with each stage. *Rabbit Guise* retracts the right wing, leaving Touka's
  one-sided silhouette (C9).
- **Era 2, *:re* (mastery 750+):** the wings are much larger (C10): about 4 blocks, roughly 1.5 times the
  *Tokyo Ghoul* span, with more flame tongues. The *One-Winged Kagune* replaces the pair with **one huge
  crystallized crescent** about 3.5 blocks tall (C15).
- **Body language:** light, upright and fast. He fights on his toes, mixing kicks with wing strikes (C8, C16),
  unlike Kaneki's heavy, low stance.
- **Not part of the kagune:** his masks (C12) and the *:re* battle suit (C11) are clothing, so the mod does not draw
  them.

## Progression (mastery-gated, like Kaneki's forms)
Mastery is shared with the existing system, so the thresholds match the Kaneki path's pacing. The thresholds
themselves are tuning; the order follows Ayato's story.

| Mastery | Stage | Canon anchor | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Awakened Ukaku** | first appearance, Anteiku (C6) | twin wings, kick + shard close-range combo, short *Shard Volley*, *Wing Block* |
| 100 | **Black Rabbit** | Aogiri arc (C9, C12) | *Rabbit Guise* (one-wing mimicry); longer volleys |
| 250 | **Wing Blades** | trained hardening (C5) | blade-wing combo; crystallized wings can now strike up close |
| 450 | **SS-Rated Executive** | SS rating (C1) | larger wings, denser volleys, lower RC drain |
| 750 | **:re Ayato** | *:re* (C10, C15) | the *:re* look (much larger wings), the *One-Winged Kagune* |
| 1000 | **Mastered :re Ayato** | *:re* stamina (C7) | the low-RC weakness is gone; the One-Winged Kagune at full power |

Control, bloodlust, hunger and the ghoul diet from 1.1.0 apply to both paths.

## Abilities
Same keys as the Kaneki path; what each key does depends on the path. Keys with no canon Ayato move do nothing on
this path.

| Key (default) | Kaneki path (unchanged) | Ayato path | Canon |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | tendril combos | **Close-range combo**: kicks and wing strikes (below) | C5, C16 |
| Rapid Strike (Z) | rapid tendril strike | **Shard Volley**: crystallize and fire bullets from both wings. Count, spread and damage grow with stage | C4 |
| Guard (C) | kagune guard | ~~**Wing Block**: one crystallized wing swings in front of you and stops melee and projectiles. Fast RC drain~~ Removed in 5.0.13: C does nothing on this path | C6, C17 |
| Cycle tendrils (K) | tail count | **Wing form**: Twin Wings → *Rabbit Guise* (one wing, 100+) → *One-Winged Kagune* (750+) | C9, C10, C15 |
| Slingshot (J), Slam (B), Lift (V), Grab (G), Throw, Crush | grapple and movement kit | **nothing**: Ayato never grapples, dives, glides or air-dashes | — |

### Close-range combo (M1)
| Wing form | Stages | Canon |
|---|---|---|
| Twin Wings, below mastery 250 | Kick → Kick → **Point-blank Shard Burst** (both wings fire at close range) | C16, C4 |
| Twin Wings, mastery 250+ | Right Wing Blade → Left Wing Blade → Kick → **Cross Cut** (both blades) | C5, C16 |
| Rabbit Guise, below mastery 250 | Kick → Kick → Point-blank Shard Burst (one side only) | C9, C16, C4 |
| Rabbit Guise, mastery 250+ | Kick → One-Wing Slash → Point-blank Shard Burst (one side only) | C9, C5, C16, C4 |
| One-Winged Kagune (750+) | One-Wing Slash → Return Slash → **Beheading Cut** (a wide sweep, the path's strongest single hit) | C15 |

Before Wing Blades the wings never strike up close: they only fire. Close combat with the wings is what Ayato
trains for (C5), so every wing cut needs mastery 250. Kicks reach about 3 blocks (legs don't grow); wing strikes
reach further as the wings grow, up to about 6.5 blocks for the One-Winged Kagune.

### Path rules (all canon)
- **Crystallized wings can't strike up close (C4):** after a Shard Volley the wings stay crystallized for about a
  second. Below mastery 250, the M1 combo can only kick during that window. *Wing Blades* (C5) lifts the limit, the
  way Ayato trained himself to.
- **Low RC supply (C7):** shards and the Wing Block drain RC fast at first. Mastery shrinks the drain step by step
  until, at 1000, it is no worse than the Kaneki path's (*:re* Ayato fights long battles).
- **Ukaku speed (C8):** Speed and Jump Boost while the wings are out. This is passive only; there is no dash.
  Speed I, Speed II from SS rank (450); Jump Boost I, Jump Boost II in the *:re* era (750). Like the Kaneki
  forms' own buffs, they are short ambient effects that never override a stronger potion.
- **Rabbit Guise (C9):** one wing is out, so shards come half as fast, RC drains more slowly and one-wing slashes
  are quicker. Everyone sees Touka's one-sided "Rabbit" silhouette.

## Visual references
The first draft of the look came from written descriptions. It was then redrawn against reference frames supplied
for this build: the *Tokyo Ghoul* season 1 flame wings, the crystallized blades from the Cochlea arc, and the *:re*
One-Winged Kagune. More references that would still help (screenshots or manga panels):
1. *Tokyo Ghoul* (anime season 1): Ayato's first fight with Touka at Anteiku, with both wings out and the one-wing
   block (C6).
2. *Tokyo Ghoul* / *√A*: Ayato firing shards from crystallized wings (C4).
3. *Tokyo Ghoul* manga or anime: Ayato as "Black Rabbit" with one wing retracted (C9).
4. *:re* (anime or manga): the Auction, with the large wings and the One-Winged Kagune in both manga versions (C10, C15).

## Starting GUI: "Choose your kagune"
The first time a player's kagune is unlocked (`/kagune unlock <player> on` or any future unlock source), a full-screen
choice opens.

```
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                       YOUR RC CELLS ARE WAKING UP                        │
 │        What tears out of your back decides how you fight. This is        │
 │                     permanent; choose with your gut.                     │
 │                                                                          │
 │  ┌───────────────────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────────────────┐  │
 │  │  KANEKI PATH                  │    │  AYATO PATH                   │  │
 │  │  Rinkaku · Centipede · Kakuja │    │  Ukaku · Black Rabbit         │  │
 │  │      (tendril silhouette)     │    │      (wing silhouette)        │  │
 │  │  • tendrils from lower back   │    │  • vermilion wings from the   │  │
 │  │  • grapple, crush, slam       │    │    shoulder blades            │  │
 │  │  • tough, regenerating        │    │  • shard volleys, wing blades │  │
 │  │  • ends in: Full Kakuja       │    │  • fast on foot and in the air│  │
 │  │                               │    │  • low RC: burst, then breathe│  │
 │  │  MELEE · GRAPPLE · TANK       │    │  • ends as :re Ayato          │  │
 │  │  0 ─ 100 ─ 250 ─ 450 ─ 1000   │    │    (One-Winged Kagune)        │  │
 │  │                               │    │  RANGED · SPEED · BLADES      │  │
 │  └───────────────────────────────┘    └───────────────────────────────┘  │
 │                                                                          │
 │                  [ hold to AWAKEN AS AYATO ]    decide later             │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Cards:** the Kaneki card is crimson on black; the Ayato card is vermilion on black. Hovering or selecting a card
  lifts it slightly, makes its border glow and animates its silhouette (tendrils writhe, wings flicker between
  gaseous and crystallized).
- **Progression strip** along the bottom of each card shows that path's stage names at their mastery thresholds.
- **Confirm by holding** the Awaken button for about 1 second, because the choice is permanent. A ring fills while you
  hold, and releasing early cancels.
- **Keyboard:** ←/→ to pick, hold Enter to confirm, Esc for "decide later".
- **Decide later** closes the screen. Until a path is chosen, trying to manifest (or pressing the HexKagune menu key)
  reopens it, so nobody gets stuck and nobody skips it by accident.
- **Server-authoritative:** the client only sends "I choose X". The server accepts it only if the player is unlocked
  and has no path yet, then saves it and syncs it to everyone.
- **Existing saves:** a player who already has kagune progress (mastery, mutations or a non-starting form) is
  quietly assigned the Kaneki path and never sees the screen. Operators can change anyone's path with
  `/kagune path set <player> <kaneki|ayato>`. `/kagune path` shows your own path.
- **Narrow windows:** below about 420 scaled pixels wide, the cards stack vertically and the text shortens.

## Implementation plan (one step at a time, each pushed)
1. ✅ This design document (canon-only, by era).
2. ✅ Path data: a stored path (unchosen / Kaneki / Ayato) in the existing profile NBT, legacy saves assigned Kaneki,
   synced to clients, `/kagune path`.
3. ✅ Starting GUI: the choice screen, a validated choice packet, and the manifest/menu gate that reopens it.
4. ✅ Ayato manifestation: the wings renderer, by era (*Tokyo Ghoul* pair, *:re* large wings, One-Winged
   Kagune), gaseous and crystallized, matched to the reference images; the Kaneki tendril renderer and writhing
   sounds skip Ayato players.
5. ✅ Close-range combo (kicks and wing strikes; server-authoritative, reuses the 1.1.0 combo system), the
   crystallized-wings rule, body animation for every strike, and RC trails (red arcs, molten shard streaks).
6. ✅ Shard Volley (projectiles): waves of crystal shards launched from points along the drawn crystallized
   blades, aimed at the target (or the crosshair), spread over a cone; the One-Winged Kagune never fires (it is a
   blade); the wings stay crystallized for a second afterwards.
7. ✅ Ukaku passives (Speed and Jump Boost while the wings are out; the RC cost curve already applies to every
   strike and volley) and the Kaneki-only mechanics switched off: grapple/grab/throw/crush/lift/slam keys and Threat
   Auto Attack refuse with a reminder, tendrils never catch Ayato's falls, and the Kaneki form, mutations and Threat
   Auto setting are parked (restored if the path changes back).
8. ✅ (removed in 5.0.13) Wing Block: the Guard key wraps the crystallized left wing around the front; hits and projectiles from the
   front (within 70° of where he faces) lose 50% of their damage (75% at mastery 1000); upkeep and every stopped
   hit cost RC (low-RC multiplier); it drops when the RC runs out, when he strikes or fires, or when the wings go.
9. ✅ Wing forms on the tail-cycle key: Twin Wings → Rabbit Guise (100+) → One-Winged Kagune (750+), stored in
   the profile, synced to everyone, with a short reshape of the wings on every client.
