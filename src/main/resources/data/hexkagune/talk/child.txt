# Every child is its own: its age (baby, toddler, child, teen), its temperament for life (sunny, shy, bold, curious, mischief, gentle,
# serious) and its mood now (happy, sleepy, scared, missed, bored, proud, sad, hurt). {call} is what it calls the parent it talks to
# (Mama, Papa; a teen's Mom, Dad), {other} the other parent, {othername} their name, {me} its own name, {sib} a brother or sister.
# A baby doesn't talk: it babbles, reaches, giggles; what the parent does is the answer.
