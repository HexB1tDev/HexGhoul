The child skins in this folder are composed at runtime from parts of the edit_skin mod for Minetest/Luanti
(https://github.com/MrRar/edit_skin, by MrRar), converted to RGBA and renamed (edit_skin_<part>.png -> <part>.png).
Each part keeps its own licence, as below (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike); the skins made from them are shared
under the same licences. Masks (<part>_mask.png) go with their part.
